What the app keeps

The app keeps data in two places: on the server (a Supabase database, so your progress follows you between devices) and on the device you use.

On the server, per account

Sign-in (Supabase Auth)
Your email address and password. Supabase checks the password when you sign in; the app itself never stores it. Supabase also keeps its own records of sign-ins.
profiles
Your email, the name you gave, whether the account is a normal account or the admin, and when it was created. Once the app asks for them (see Young learners): the learner's year of birth, whether a parent or guardian has agreed, and that parent's or guardian's email.
entitlements
Whether the account has access and why (none, active, trialing, past due, cancelled, or free access given by the admin), the plan (monthly, annual or free), Stripe's customer and subscription reference numbers, when the paid period ends, and whether it is set to cancel. Written only by the payment system, never by the app.
snapshots
One copy of the account's progress. For each learner on the account: their name, settings (including theory test date, daily goal, voice and reminder choices), every practice and mock answer (which question, when, right or wrong, and which option was picked), memory boxes, mock test results, flags, XP, streak, notes, and a profile photo if one was uploaded (reduced to 128 × 128 pixels). Also any question edits, question-pack choices and learner access settings made on the admin dashboard.
events
The activity log: one row for each thing a learner does — each answer (with the option picked and how long it took), each question shown, practice sessions started, finished or left, mock tests started, finished or abandoned, changed mock answers, hints, read-aloud, flags and un-flags, screens opened, "More like this", finishing or skipping the quick setup, dismissing the What's new card, and memory-tip reviews. Each row has the learner's name and the time on the device. Rows are only ever added; the app cannot change or delete them.
reminders
If you switch on daily reminders: whether they are on, the hour you chose, your time-zone offset, the last day you practised and the last day you were reminded.
push_subs
If reminders are on and server reminders are set up: the address your browser gives for delivering notifications, its two security keys, and your browser's identifying text (the first 200 characters).
questions
The question bank. Not personal data.

On your device

The app saves your progress, settings, sign-in session and a copy of the question bank in your browser's storage so it works offline and opens quickly. It sets no cookies of its own. The full list is on the cookies and storage page.

What the app does not do

  • No adverts, and no third-party analytics or tracking code.
  • No card details: when card payments are live, you enter them on Stripe's own page, never in the app.
  • Your data is not sold or shared for marketing.

Why it is kept

  • Your account and progress — to sign you in and give you the same progress on every device.
  • The activity log — so the coach can build lessons from your answers, and so the adult helping you (the admin) can see what you have practised and what you find hard.
  • Access and payments — to know whether the account may read the full question bank.
  • Reminders — to nudge you only on days you haven't practised, at the time you chose.

The lawful basis for each, under UK GDPR Article 6(1):

  • Your account, progress, the coach, and access and payments — contract (6(1)(b)): they are the service you signed up for.
  • The admin seeing learners' activity — legitimate interests (6(1)(f)): so the adult helping a learner can see what they have practised and what they find hard.
  • Reminders — consent (6(1)(a)): nothing is kept until you switch them on, and you can switch them off at any time.

Who can see it

  • You — each account can read only its own rows. This is enforced by the database itself (row-level security), not just by the app.
  • The admin — the admin account can read every account's activity log and profile (that is how the Activity screen works), and edit the question bank.
  • The operator — whoever runs the Supabase project can see everything in the database from Supabase's dashboard. That is the operator named at the top of this notice, who looks only to answer your requests, fix problems and keep the app running.
  • The services below, only as far as they need to do their job.

Outside the UK

The database is in Germany. UK law treats every EU country as giving adequate protection, so no extra safeguard is needed for it (ICO: adequacy regulations).

The other services above may handle what they receive outside the UK, for example in the United States. Each one's privacy policy, linked above, sets out the safeguards it relies on; those have not been checked one by one for this sample.

How long it is kept

  • Everything above is kept for as long as the account exists. Nothing is deleted automatically before then.
  • The progress copy (snapshots) is replaced each time it syncs, so it only ever holds the latest version.
  • The activity log grows: rows are added, never changed.
  • Deleting an account removes its rows from every table above, because each one is linked to the account and set to be deleted with it.
  • What the app saves on your device stays there until you clear it (see the cookies and storage page).
  • Records the services below keep for themselves, such as Supabase's sign-in records and Stripe's payment records, follow their own policies.

Getting a copy, or having it deleted

A copy of your data

In the app: Admin dashboard → Data → Export backup downloads a file with the learner's settings, memory boxes, answers and mock tests.

The server can also produce a complete copy of everything it holds for your account — profile, access, progress, reminder settings, the devices set up for reminders, and the whole activity log — but the button for it in the app is not built yet. Until it is, ask using the contact above.

Deleting data

The server can delete an account and all its rows from every table when the account's owner asks, but the button for it in the app is not built yet. It will refuse, and say why, for:

  • the admin account (another account must be made the admin first), and
  • an account whose subscription is still set to renew (cancel it first with Manage subscription).

Until the button exists, ask using the contact above, and the operator will delete just the activity history, or the whole account.

Settings → Reset all progress in the app clears a learner's answers, memory boxes and mock scores, but not the activity log on the server.

Requests are answered within one month, the time UK data protection law usually allows (ICO).

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, and object to or limit how it is used. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Young learners

Learners may be under 18. The app's rule is that a learner who may be younger than 16 needs a parent's or guardian's agreement. The server can record a learner's year of birth, whether that agreement was given, and the parent's or guardian's email, but the app does not ask for them yet.

Until it does: a learner younger than 16 should ask a parent or guardian before signing up. Full access is given by the admin by hand, and the admin checks that a parent or guardian has agreed before giving it to a learner that young. A parent or guardian who finds an account they did not agree to can ask for it to be deleted, using the contact above.
