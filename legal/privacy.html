What the app keeps

The app keeps data in two places: on the server (a Supabase database, so your progress follows you between devices) and on the device you use.

On the server, per account

Sign-in (Supabase Auth)
Your email address and password. Supabase checks the password when you sign in; the app itself never stores it. Supabase also keeps its own records of sign-ins.
profiles
Your email, the name you gave, whether the account is a normal account or the admin, and when it was created.
entitlements
Whether the account has access and why (none, active, trialing, past due, cancelled, or free access given by the admin), the plan (monthly, annual or free), Stripe's customer and subscription reference numbers, when the paid period ends, and whether it is set to cancel. Written only by the payment system, never by the app.
snapshots
One copy of the account's progress. For each learner on the account: their name, settings (including theory test date, daily goal, voice and reminder choices), every practice and mock answer (which question, when, right or wrong, and which option was picked), memory boxes, mock test results, flags, XP, streak, notes, and a profile photo if one was uploaded (reduced to 128 × 128 pixels). Also any question edits, question-pack choices and learner access settings made on the admin dashboard.
events
The activity log: one row for each thing a learner does — each answer (with the option picked and how long it took), each question shown, practice sessions started, finished or left, mock tests started, finished or abandoned, changed mock answers, hints, read-aloud, flags and un-flags, screens opened, "More like this" and memory-tip reviews. Each row has the learner's name and the time on the device. Rows are only ever added; the app cannot change or delete them.
reminders
If you switch on daily reminders: whether they are on, the hour you chose, your time-zone offset, the last day you practised and the last day you were reminded.
push_subs
If reminders are on and server reminders are set up: the address your browser gives for delivering notifications, its two security keys, and your browser's identifying text (the first 200 characters).
questions
The question bank. Not personal data.

On your device

The app saves your progress, settings, sign-in session and a copy of the question bank in your browser's storage so it works offline and opens quickly. It sets no cookies of its own. The full list is on the cookies and storage page.

What the app does not do

  • No adverts, and no third-party analytics or tracking code.
  • No card details: when card payments are live, you enter them on Stripe's own page, never in the app.
  • Your data is not sold or shared for marketing.

Why it is kept

  • Your account and progress — to sign you in and give you the same progress on every device.
  • The activity log — so the coach can build lessons from your answers, and so the adult helping you (the admin) can see what you have practised and what you find hard.
  • Access and payments — to know whether the account may read the full question bank.
  • Reminders — to nudge you only on days you haven't practised, at the time you chose.

Who can see it

  • You — each account can read only its own rows. This is enforced by the database itself (row-level security), not just by the app.
  • The admin — the admin account can read every account's activity log and profile (that is how the Activity screen works), and edit the question bank.
  • The operator — whoever runs the Supabase project can see everything in the database from Supabase's dashboard: [to be filled in by Darren].
  • The services below, only as far as they need to do their job.

Services that handle data for the app

Your browser's notification service
Only if server reminders are set up and you switch reminders on: the company that makes your browser or phone delivers the nudge.

How long it is kept

  • There is no automatic deletion today: everything above is kept until it is deleted.
  • The progress copy (snapshots) is replaced each time it syncs, so it only ever holds the latest version.
  • The activity log grows: rows are added, never changed.
  • Deleting an account removes its rows from every table above, because each one is linked to the account and set to be deleted with it.

Getting a copy, or having it deleted

A copy of your data

In the app: Admin dashboard → Data → Export backup downloads a file with the learner's settings, memory boxes, answers and mock tests. For everything the server holds (including the activity log), ask using the contact above.

Deleting data

There is no delete button in the app yet. Ask using the contact above, and the operator will:

  • delete just the activity history, or
  • delete the whole account, which removes it and all its rows from every table.

Settings → Reset all progress in the app clears a learner's answers, memory boxes and mock scores, but not the activity log on the server.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, and object to or limit how it is used. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
