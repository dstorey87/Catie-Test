What the app is — and isn't

  • A practice app for the multiple-choice part of the UK driving theory test. It does not include hazard perception.
  • It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the DVSA (Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency) or any government body.
  • Its questions are original practice questions in the same format and topics as the real test — not the DVSA's own question bank, which is licensed and not published.
  • The official rules are in the Highway Code on GOV.UK. If the app and the Highway Code disagree, the Highway Code is right.

Accuracy

The questions, answers and explanations are written carefully and checked against the Highway Code, but mistakes are possible and rules change. The readiness score and mock results are a guide to how your preparation is going, not a promise that you will pass the real test.

If you find a mistake, please tell us using the contact above.

Limits of our responsibility: the app is a practice aid. We are not responsible for the result of your theory test, or for loss caused by relying on the app instead of the Highway Code. Nothing in these terms takes away your legal rights as a consumer, or limits any responsibility the law does not allow to be limited.

Age: there is no minimum age, but a learner younger than 16 may use the app only with a parent's or guardian's agreement. How that works until the app asks for it is in the privacy notice.

Refunds are covered by the refund policy. Price changes for existing subscribers: we tell you at least 30 days before a new price applies to you. It starts at your first renewal after that notice, and you can cancel before then with Manage subscription.
