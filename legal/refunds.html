How to stop a subscription, and when you can have your money back.

What you pay

The prices shown in the app are £4.99 a month or £50 a year, taken by Stripe. A subscription renews by itself at the end of each month or year until you cancel it.

Cancelling

  1. In the app, tap Manage subscription (on the lock screen, or in Admin dashboard → Account).
  2. Stripe's page opens. Cancel there.

Cancelling stops the next payment. You keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for, and it then switches off by itself. There's nothing else to do and no one to email.

Refunds

Changing your mind

UK law gives buyers of digital services 14 days to cancel and get a refund, unless they agreed at checkout to start straight away and gave up that right. The app's checkout does not ask for that agreement yet.
