## Changelog
- Two tabs (#51): with the app open in one tab and Adventure (or the app again) in another, the app tab now shows her new answers, goal ring, streak, XP, flags and stars the moment the other tab saves, with no reload. Hearing a save never saves anything back, so two open tabs settle straight away.
- The app no longer saves its copy of her record whole. When another tab has saved since this one last read or saved, the two are joined: answers by question and time (sign answers too), flags question by question (the later flag or un-flag wins), Adventure stars stage by stage, XP added up, and the goal days of both. Nothing Adventure or another app tab saved is lost, even a save the app had not heard yet. "Reset all progress" and "Import backup" still replace her answers, and a tab that missed a reset does not bring the wiped answers back.
- The app uses the rules it shares with Adventure (coach.js) instead of its own copies: streak and daily goal, reading style (text size, easy-reading font, high contrast), which voice reads aloud, and the words read out.
- Fixed: "Read the explanation aloud" ended with the word "undefined" when a question had no rule reference (or the plain re-wording was open but none was written).
- Admin → Activity (#57) now also lists: notes added, changed or deleted (which screen and question, and how long, never the words), each Settings change (old → new, in the Settings screen's own words, never a name), printing (which format, how many questions, and for flashcards which ones), and Adventure's stages ("Finished an Adventure stage in world 2: 6/7 right, 1 star").
- The first open after an update, while an older offline coach.js is still in use, no longer risks a blank page: coaching is simply off for that one open.

## Status
- What exists: the app hears another tab's saves (`heardSave`, the `storage` event) and joins its saves with the stored copy when another tab saved in between (`TTAdv.join` in the page head); otherwise a save is exactly as before. Notes, Settings changes and printing are tracked (`trackNote`, `trackSettings`, `trackPrint`); the Settings and Print choices and the words Activity uses for them live in one list (`TTChoices` in the page head).
- How to test: `node --test` (453). In a browser, open the app through `tests/browser/harness.js` and `adventure.html` as a second page of the SAME context (`ctx.newPage()`, so they share storage); play a stage in Adventure and the app's Home goal ring, XP and Adventure stars change without a reload. Route `**/rest/v1/events**` to keep what is POSTed and return it on GET to see the new lines on Admin → Activity (`role: 'admin'`). Headless `window.print()` fires `beforeprint`, so the Print button is testable.
- Left: a stale app tab that missed another tab's save can still show old numbers until her next save or the next save it hears (then it shows everything). A test in progress in one app tab is not joined with another app tab's (the tab that saves last wins that one part, as before). Printing a screen other than Print through the browser's own menu is recorded as "Printed <screen>", with no count.
