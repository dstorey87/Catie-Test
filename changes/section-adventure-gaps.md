## Changelog
- **Adventure answers count toward the daily goal and the streak exactly like practice (#47).** Every Adventure answer (a second chance too, as a drill repeat in practice) goes into the learner's `attempts` in the app's shape with `src:'adventure'`, adds its XP, and now also updates her streak record as the app's `award()` does after a practice answer: today goes on her goal-day list once her answers today reach her goal, and the app's day counter (`todayDate`/`todayN`) moves on. She is also marked active today for the reminder server (`TTPush.markActive`), so she is not nudged to keep a streak she has already kept. (The goal ring already counted Adventure attempts on reopening the app; what was missing was the streak record and the active mark.)
- **Adventure follows her reading settings**: text size (zoom 1 / 1.12 / 1.25), the easy-reading font (Lexend, now loaded on the page), high contrast, reduce motion, and light/dark. Headings keep Nunito, as in the app; the question, options and explanation use her font.
- **Read aloud in Adventure**, as in practice: a speaker beside the question (reads it with its options in the order on screen), beside each option and beside the explanation; with "Read questions aloud automatically" on, each question and the result are read out. Same voice (her chosen English voice, else the best-sounding one) and speed as the app; each read is logged as `read_aloud` for Activity. A device with no speech gets no speaker buttons.
- **Two tabs**: an app tab left open while she plays Adventure can save its older copy of her record over Adventure's answers, XP and flags. Adventure now remembers what it saved while open and, when it hears that save (the browser's `storage` event), puts back anything dropped, in time order with its XP and goal day. A later flag or un-flag in the app still wins, and after "Reset progress" nothing is brought back. Adventure also follows a text-size, font, contrast or theme change made in the app tab straight away.
- The rules both pages need now live once in `coach.js`: `dailyGoal`, `recordGoalDay`, `streakAward`, `READING` + `readingStyle`, `pickVoice`, `sayQuestion`, `sayAnswer`, `sayExplanation` (and `localDay` is exported). `tests/adventure-page.test.js` runs the app's own `award()`, reading-style lines, `bestVoice()`, `speakQFull` and spoken-result code and checks the coach.js copies give the same answers.
- Help → Adventure mode says all of this in one paragraph.
