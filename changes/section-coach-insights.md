## Changelog
- Coach (`coach.js`): **pass prediction** (`passPrediction`): gives the expected mock score out of 50 and the chance of reaching 43. It works from her accuracy in each topic, with recent answers counting more (an answer 14 days older than her newest counts half as much). A topic she has barely tried is pulled toward her overall rate. Each topic's rate is multiplied by how many questions that topic gets in the app's own "Like the real test" mock (4 each for topics 1–8, 3 each for 9–14). The pass chance is worked out exactly (Poisson-binomial), not sampled. It also returns how many answers it rests on, so the screen can say "not enough answers yet" below one mock's worth (50). It is deterministic: no clock and no randomness.
- Coach: **what to work on** (`improvements`): ranks topics by predicted gain, with a ready-made sentence such as "You lose about 1.2 of the 4 marks on Safety margins in a mock — about 10 more right answers here would add ~0.4 to your expected score." The gain comes from rerunning the same prediction with those 10 answers added. It is never a separate estimate.
- Coach: **study plan** (`studyPlan`): builds a day-by-day plan up to the day before the test, with a daily question target and two focus topics a day, rotated best first. The workload is her own daily goal × the days in the plan, raised if needed so every question she has never seen fits before test day. A missed day's questions are spread over the days left, and a day's target never drops below her goal. It handles no test date, a date already gone, and test day itself.
- Coach: **misconceptions** (`misconceptions`, the distractor analysis): for each question, the wrong option she keeps choosing (2+ times), with its count and share, in the bank's own words. For each topic, her three most-chosen wrong options.
- Coach: **badge closeness** (`badgeCloseness`): shows which badge is nearest and how many more of what earns it ("10 more questions earns Century"). It takes the app's `badgeList()` as it is. "Ten in a row" and the streak badges count from her current run or streak, not her best ever.
- Coach: **streak freeze** (`streakWithFreeze`): each missed day uses up one earned freeze and the streak survives (she earns one freeze per 7 goal days and can hold at most 2). Freezes are worked out from the days alone, so there is nothing new to store or sync.
- Coach: `dayCounts` (answers per local day, which feeds the plan and the streak) and `mockMix`. Every threshold lives in one `COACH` object (`TTCoach.coachDefaults`), like `DRILL`.
- 35 new tests in `tests/coach.test.js`: 90 in the suite now. Mutation-checked: 12 deliberate bugs, each caught by the test meant to catch it.
- `coach.js` is in `sw.js`'s CORE list, so bump `VERSION` at promotion.

## Status
- `coach.js` now also answers: expected mock score and pass chance, what to work on, a study plan to the test date, the wrong answers she keeps picking, the nearest badge, and a streak that earned freezes can save. All six are pure functions on `window.TTCoach`. To try them in the browser console: `TTCoach.passPrediction(attempts, tests, bank)`. To test: `node --test`.
- Left: the screens (App/UI lane). Home should show the prediction plus the top one or two `improvements` sentences, with a "not enough answers yet" state. A test-date setting should feed `studyPlan`. My answers or Admin should show `misconceptions`. Badges and the streak flame should show `badgeCloseness` and the freeze count. The app's stored streak (`state.streak`, UTC days) would switch to `streakWithFreeze(goal days from dayCounts)` (local days).
- The tuning values (14-day half-life, 3-answer blending, 50-answer minimum, 10-answer what-if, 7-day freeze, max 2) are choices, not measurements. None has been checked against real test results (not verified).
