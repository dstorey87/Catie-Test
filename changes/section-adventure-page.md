## Changelog
- New page `adventure.html` (Adventure mode, issue #27): a Duolingo-style route through the 14 theory test topics. Each topic is a world, drawn as a winding road with its stages on it: round lesson stops and a checkpoint flag (a trophy once passed). Locked stages are grey with a padlock and say what to pass first; the stage she is up to pulses, with a little car on it and a "Start" label; passed stages show 1 to 3 stars. World tabs and arrows move between worlds; the top bar shows her stars out of all there are.
- Playing a stage: a progress bar, one question at a time with its options in a new order each play, and an instant feedback panel: green "Nice!" with +10 XP, or red "Not quite" with the right answer. Both show the bank's explanation and rule reference, plus the memory tip when the admin has approved one. A combo counter shows right answers in a row. A question she gets wrong comes back once before the stage ends; the score counts first tries only. Answer with a tap, or the keys 1-4 / A-D; Enter moves on. Quit asks first ("Keep going" / "Leave").
- A Flag button on every question uses the app's own flags (`revisionFlags[qid] = {t, src: 'adventure'}`; un-flagging sets `flagCleared[qid]`), so flagged questions show in the app's Flagged screen and sync the same way.
- Results: score, stars that pop in, pass or fail against the pass mark with encouraging words, confetti on a pass (none when the device or the app's Reduce motion setting asks for less movement), and Next stage / Try again / Map. A pass that opens the next world says so.
- Saved into the learner chosen in the app (`theoryTrainer.d.<learnerId>`): each answer is added to `attempts` (`src: 'adventure'`, and `p` is the option she picked in the question's own order; the last 3000 are kept, like the app), +10 XP for each right answer (like the app), progress under `adventure` using `TTCoach.adventureRecord` (best score and stars kept, a pass stays a pass), and `updatedAt`. Events go to the same queue as the app: `answer` (with `src: 'adventure'`, `stage`, `ms`), `flag` / `unflag`, `adventure_stage_start` and `adventure_stage_end`.
- It uses the same bank as the app (`TTBank.load()`: server, then the offline copy, then the free sample) with the admin's deletions and corrections applied. On the free sample it says "Sign in to the app to unlock every world". With no learner chosen yet it sends her to the app first.
- No adventure number is typed into the page: the pass mark, stars and lesson and checkpoint sizes all come from `TTCoach.ADVENTURE` in `coach.js`.
- Light and dark follow the app's Appearance choice (`tt.theme`) or the device, in the app's colours; works at 390px and 1280px; real buttons with a visible focus ring, a skip link and screen-reader messages.
- 21 new tests in `tests/adventure-page.test.js` (the page's saving, flags, comebacks, scoring, map maths, and checks that the XP and attempts numbers still match the app and no adventure number is hard-coded).
