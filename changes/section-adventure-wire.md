## Changelog
- Home has an **Adventure** card just under Today's lesson (issue #27): a little map picture, the world she is on ("World 3 · Safety and your vehicle"), how many of that world's stages she has passed, and her stars out of every star on the route. Tapping it opens `adventure.html` in the same tab. Before she has played it says "Start your road trip through 14 topics"; once every stage is passed, "Every world done". The numbers are counted by `coach.js` (`adventureRoute` over the same questions the Adventure page uses, and `adventureStatus` over her saved progress); the words come from `TTAdv.card` in the page head.
- Fix: opening the app no longer wipes Adventure progress. `persist()` rewrote the learner's whole record from the app's state and dropped `adventure`; it now reads the stored copy just before writing and carries `adventure` over (`TTAdv.keep`, list `TTAdv.KEEP`), so the page's latest save always survives, even with the app open in another tab.
- The answers, XP and flags the Adventure page adds are picked up when the app opens (the first load and `loadUser` both read them from her record, as before). New: coming back with the browser's Back button, when the browser shows the app from its back/forward cache without reloading it, the app re-reads her record (`pageshow`), so it doesn't save over what the page added.
- `questions()` is split: `bankQuestions()` is the bank with the admin's deletions and corrections (what Adventure uses), and `questions()` adds the hand-added ones to it. One copy of that logic.
- The How-to guide (`help.html`) has an **Adventure mode** section: what it is, how stages and worlds unlock, the pass mark and stars, replaying, flags and where progress is saved, with a screenshot of the map at 390px. A contents link and a line in the Home list point to it.
- The app keeps its own copy of the 14 topic names (`this.TOPICS`) instead of using `TTCoach.TOPIC_NAMES`: `tests/coach.test.js` (Coach lane) reads the app's array to check the two match, and the app would lose every topic name if `coach.js` failed to load. That test already fails if the two copies ever differ.
- 8 new tests in `tests/app-files.test.js`: persist and loadUser (lifted from the page and run against a fake localStorage) keep the page's answers, XP, flags and progress; the latest progress wins; the card's words for a new learner, part-way, a finished world and everything done, against the real `coach.js` and bank; the card's place and wiring on Home; the Back-button re-read; and the guide's numbers match `TTCoach.ADVENTURE`.
